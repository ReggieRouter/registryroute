Why this page is on Registry Route

Registry Route began as an internal routing sheet: fifty Secretary of State portals, each with its own URL scheme, its own search form and its own idea of what a business record is. It was published free because the underwriting desks that needed it should not have to keep rebuilding it.

The routing directory is the part you can see. Behind it sits the structuring, the link verification and the interface work, and that is the same work LendPaper does for lenders, funders and ISOs on their own systems. If a piece of your process looks like a spreadsheet that only one person knows how to run, that is the conversation.
