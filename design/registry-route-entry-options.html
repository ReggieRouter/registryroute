How do you enter a company?

Three simplified options · click the states to feel each one

Same map, same tiers (🟢 Instant · 🔵 Pre-fill ⚡ · ⬜ Manual). The only thing that differs is how you tell Registry Route which company to check. Pick the flow that feels most obvious — bias to easiness over features.

Option A · type-once, persistent chips

Chips under the bar

One input. Enter makes a chip. Paste a list → many chips. The green chip is who we check; click a state to check it. (This is the current v4.)
Press Enter to add · paste a list for several · click a chip to make it active.

No global bar
